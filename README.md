### Hi, I'm Giovanni 👋

🤖 **AI Engineer** in Milan. At [Quantyca](https://www.quantyca.it) I build AI platforms for enterprises: governed registries of MCP servers and A2A agents, RAG over regulated documentation, and the data pipelines underneath them.

🔐 Before that I did research on privacy attacks against federated learning at Politecnico di Milano. [**TimberStrike**](https://doi.org/10.56553/popets-2025-0145) (PoPETs 2025) shows that a single honest-but-curious client can reconstruct other participants' training data from federated gradient-boosted trees ([code](https://github.com/necst/TimberStrike)).
